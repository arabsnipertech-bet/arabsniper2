# Bounded nightly Auditor recovery

Updated November 6, 2026. Selection thresholds, market settlement formulas and the V5.1 freeze are unchanged.

- Production admission is 00:30–01:30 and 04:00–05:10 Europe/Rome (end exclusive). All phases are forcibly bounded by 01:50 or 05:30, leaving the scheduled 02:00 Night Scan and 06:00 Fast Scan clear. An active or queued data writer prevents admission.
- Automatic schedules are 00:30 and 04:30. The existing external Auditor watchdog can resume partial work in the same permitted windows. Manual recovery uses the identical admission gate and cannot bypass it during the day.
- Yesterday has priority over any older batch: reconstruct only that closed date from its existing archive and pending forecast/policy journals, settle with the normal engine, update V5.1 calibration and save a separate priority receipt. Journals are neither deleted nor consumed by this pass, and the historical batch/cursor remains intact. A priority receipt is not full-history certification.
- Preparation consumes up to eight immutable scan journals per invocation, before the frozen batch's midnight cutoff. Later scans remain pending for the next batch. The legacy combined forecast archive remains updated; only dates touched by the consumed journals are repartitioned.
- Historical journal preparation is checkpointed as `preparing` independently of the priority daily pass. A successful partial run emits `completed=false` and `paused=true`, never the full-completion proof expected by the watchdog.
- Historical recovery has no fixed date count: it uses the remaining protected time and publishes R2 after each date. A per-date receipt binds the input pointer and daily report, outcomes, Markdown and calibration outputs by SHA-256. Changed evidence triggers recalculation; missing or changed saved outputs stop certification.
- The whole admitted run has at most 75 minutes including restore and priority work, additionally capped by the unchanged protected window boundary. A new history date or final assembly requires at least 20 minutes remaining; the margin increases to 125% of observed block duration plus five minutes when that is larger. A hard timeout may require repeating only work since the latest committed checkpoint.
- Completed terminal dates can be reused across batches. Pending outcomes retain their original status and the existing 14-day lookup limit. Aggregate reports are assembled after all required date receipts are available. Full certification is written only after aggregate/comparison/calibration and final publication succeed.
