# One daily Telegram live

The external Fast Backup checks every ten minutes for eligible published picks.
It starts one long-running GitHub job, which polls API-Football every 60 seconds.
GitHub is not relaunched per minute. Existing schedule/digest and model criteria
are unchanged. No Facebook live notifications are sent.

Only actual sent-receipt picks qualify, never masked previews. A new selection
must be unstarted and within 20 minutes of kickoff. Markets: over25_ft/over35_ft;
odds 1.70–3.00, finite model probability. Among available candidates rank by
probability descending, then distance to odds 2.00, then Gold/Blu/Platinum.
The league's event coverage must be enabled and fixture status NS. This is the
best available published pick near kickoff, not a retrospective best of the day.

One reservation is stored per Rome date in private R2 under telegram-live/.
The published market and odds remain frozen. Conditional R2 writes and global
workflow concurrency fence overlapping jobs. After interruption the external
backup can resume the checkpoint; recovery may take ten minutes plus queue time.
A delivery with ambiguous Telegram acknowledgement blocks automatic resend and
fails the job for manual receipt reconciliation. Exactly-once delivery cannot be
guaranteed across Telegram and R2; preserving the pending outbox avoids blindly
resending an uncertain notification.

Two observations at least 45 seconds apart confirm a changed score or final
result. Goal minute/team are shown only when available events match the score.
Otherwise the caption gives the observed match minute. Provider corrections
produce orange notices, including reversal after the green animation. The
monitor continues after a win until ten minutes after the final score. Extra-time
and penalties do not count: use regulation fulltime score. Canceled/abandoned
fixtures are not labeled lost. Three failed reads trigger an orange delayed-feed
notice using explicitly labeled last known data. All events link to the original
channel prediction. Current minute is shown on event messages, not spammed every
minute. Poll cadence and provider freshness are not a delivery-time guarantee.

Assets are original code-rendered looping GIFs, generated by telegram_live_art.py
and attached as an Actions artifact on deployment. Push verifies permissions and
read-only access; workflow_dispatch performs actual monitoring. The Fast Backup
is the external trigger, so there is no additional cron to configure.


## Annuncio prima del calcio d’inizio
La scelta viene riservata in R2 durante la preparazione del post: prima la singola gratuita, poi le due riservate effettive, con probabilità stimata decrescente. Restano i mercati FT supportati e quote 1,70–3,00. Il monitor parte solo dopo una ricevuta Telegram che corrisponde all’annuncio. Nessuna scelta retroattiva.
Le riservate mostrano numero e orario nella grafica. Il live Gold annuncia squadre e orario, ma non mercato o quota: la selezione originale rimane nel registro privato, con un impegno SHA-256 salato nell’annuncio. Si comunicano sia esiti positivi sia negativi. Le ricevute pubbliche non contengono mercato, quota o sale delle riservate.
