# Gold and Explorer: positive alternatives and saved qualifications

When the Consigliata had no native positive candidate, a documented negative-EV
recovery could occupy it before an already-qualified positive alternative was
considered. The alternative pass now runs first. Existing qualified native
choices keep priority; recovery remains available when no admissible positive
alternative exists. Odds bands, probability estimates, role floors, compatibility,
uniqueness and Gold/Platinum admission remain unchanged.

`TRIAD_POSITIVE_ALTERNATIVES_V2` identifies new snapshots and positive alternatives.
The cached refresh emits aggregate policy/selection counts without private picks.
Already-closed snapshots and their explanations are never reclassified.

Confirmation now records a qualification type and specific missing/failed reasons.
Documented recovery has its own label and retains original negative EV. No missing
multi-operator flow is added as a BLU/Gold rejection. The shared presentation reads
saved qualification fields and never derives admission from displayed numbers.

Gold retains its three slots and adds a per-match Explorer link. Explorer can open
the same fixture in its existing search, with an explicit clear-filter button.
The official Consigliate view, historical references, favorites, outcomes and
ranking algorithms are preserved. Neither page fetches football provider data.

The existing GG FT same-rules trial remains private. The added
`nov24_away_symmetric` trial removes the away-shot direction gate only for
4.50-7.50; live routing keeps its original gate. Both trials share saved inputs,
with unchanged probabilities, EV and prices. The shadow configuration has a new
routing revision/hash; no absent historical variant is reconstructed.

Verification: routing precedence and fallback; unchanged native positive choices
and numeric estimates; immutable inputs; recovery labels and rejection reasons;
shadow-only away admission; shared rendering and fixture links; existing Python,
Node and browser regression suites. Browser screenshots use synthetic QA data.
