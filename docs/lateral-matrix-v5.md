# Universal lateral observation V5

Active engine: `3appdays.py` 3.0.0 → `policy_integration` → `statistics_integration(matrix_mode=True)`.
Model identity: `lateral-matrix-v5.0`; prospective validation starts 26 October 2026.

Every future NS/TBD fixture returned in the requested API-Football daily schedule is admitted regardless of country, tier, youth/women/reserve classification, previous quality score, price availability or history coverage. Fixtures whose data collection fails remain visible with explicit prior-based estimates. Existing started forecasts remain immutable; a started fixture with no prematch snapshot has status/results only, never a retrospective prediction. API schedule outages cannot reveal fixtures not returned by the provider; coverage is then unknown, not zero.

Statistics retain V4's recency, home/away context, robust peak control, observed xG blend and league shrinkage. Missing data use disclosed league/general priors. Droughts do not create a debt or guaranteed rebound. Four team/half Poisson rates imply coherent FT, HT and SH grids. A shared joint score distribution supplies 1X2, DC, DNB, Over/Under, BTTS, team totals, quarter/whole Asian totals/handicaps, multigoal ranges, HT/FT and other goal combos. Around 300 base outcomes are computed; every additional supported actually priced line/outcome is added. Corners, cards and player props are not inferred from goal rates.

Overlapping same-match probabilities are not multiplied. Only disjoint HT/SH increments are independent under the explicitly stated model. For Asian settlements, let W and L denote expected fractions of stake won/lost: fair decimal price is `1 + L/W`; theoretical return at price q is `W(q−1)−L`. Quarter lines split stake equally between adjacent half/whole lines. Missing HT scores stay unknown.

Each row records probability, leave-one-history-out sensitivity, descriptive historical frequency, sample quality, observed price/source timestamp, fair model price and price-return sensitivity. Sensitivity is not a confidence interval. xG is not invented when absent; no odds enter statistical rates.

Quote identity requires the verified provider bet ID, exact market name, period, side and line. Margins require a complete mutually exclusive partition from one bookmaker and provider snapshot. DC/range markets overlap: their prices are usable, but a margin is not fabricated. DNB/Asian normalized price indices have a conditional settlement basis, not an unconditional event-probability basis. Complete operators are normalized separately. Opening means **first observed**, not certified market open.

Compatible V3/V4 operator histories are retained. A row's OCI uses the same market, operators and comparable observation window, with magnitude, speed, persistence, consensus and prior OCI. Repeated identical provider snapshots do not manufacture additional observations. Complete quote partitions and actual sources remain attached to the ledger. A fixed indicative OCI magnitude of 40 is an observation threshold, not calibrated predictive certainty. Compression/expansion uses ±1 margin point. Low margin is below 5% for main FT outcome/total markets and below 7% for secondary markets; above 9% is high. These are display heuristics, not empirical liquidity measurements.

Classification separates price direction from evidence quality. Positive modeled value with positive OCI is convergence; with negative OCI, divergence. Stable positive value and sufficient actual history distinguish mature/contrarian candidates. Uncertain positive edges remain visible in observation. Combo value is based on its actual price; no synthetic multiplied quote. Volatility and related-line movements are contextual observations, never transferred as a probability bonus or direct confirmation. Aggio/OCI cannot establish confidence, liquidity, smart money, betting volume or the cause of a move.

The Gold dashboard defaults to the entire schedule, ordered by kickoff, with family/period/behavior filters and expandable full matrices. There is no top-five or fixture cap. Public homepage retains a three-example preview of the private product and states that the full schedule is available in Gold. No auth/payment changes. Live updates affect score DOM slots only.

Each fixture shows the familiar 1X2 opening/current price board, source, directional arrows and movement-strength colors (green below 3%, yellow from 3% to below 8%, red at least 8%). The opening is the first observed price of that same bookmaker. Over/HT/GG reference prices remain visible. The proposal box uses `playable-value-v1` from `src/opportunity_policy.py`; the browser consumes its eligibility decisions and ranks. All fixtures and full matrices remain available. Markets with recent actual prices 1.40–3.50 inclusive and supported statistical history qualify only when central probability is at least 35% for primary FT markets (1X2, DC, BTTS, OU1.5/2.5), or 40% for other supported markets. Primary FT markets always rank before partials/Asian/team totals, then combos/ranges. Exact score and HT/FT never enter this box. Among the same tier, rank by lower sensitivity EV minus its required cushion, then central EV, probability and ID. No eligible proposal means an explicit empty state.

The policy uses the **selected bookmaker's complete same-market overround**, not the median of other operators. Low margin (<5% primary, <7% secondary) requires EV >=2% and lower sensitivity EV >0; medium requires >=4% and >=1%; high (>9%) requires >=max(8%, margin−1) and >=2%. Expansion >=1 margin point adds 2 points to required EV and 1 to the lower sensitivity floor. Unknown/overlapping margins (e.g. DC) require >=10% and >=3%, without a confirmed trend label. Negative or >30% margins exclude a proposal, not its matrix row. These are provisional product constraints, not fitted win probabilities.

Convergence/Contrarian proposal labels require an eligible statistical price edge, known non-anomalous margin, |OCI| >=40, comparable histories on at least two agreeing operators and a strict majority, coherent margin-adjusted direction, and the selected operator moving in that same direction with persistence >=0.5 and no reversal in the observation window. Otherwise the proposal remains explicitly **flow unconfirmed**. OCI is an odds index; neither it nor margin establishes traded liquidity or smart money. Global matrix movement labels remain descriptive, with separate per-row reasons explaining box exclusions. UI filters never change a fixture's best proposal.

Price/UI-only pushes rebuild these readings from the saved private ledger, without API calls or newly invented timestamps. Scheduled/manual Fast scans continue normal statistical and price collection. Existing started forecasts stay frozen; prior snapshots without the policy never receive a fabricated historical proposal.

Every Fast recomputes future statistical profiles and current matrix readings using available source data. API-Football prematch odds may update only about every three hours, so a Fast does not imply newly changed provider prices. Source timestamps and stale/missing prices are explicit. No new paid service or model API is added; existing provider quotas still apply.

First and latest snapshots are versioned separately. The matrix audit evaluates all observed markets, including non-candidates, by exact market, family, category, behavior, margin band/trend and sample quality. Returns include Asian refunds; binary Brier/log loss exclude Asian/DNB settlement distributions. Distinct-fixture counts prevent treating hundreds of correlated markets as independent fixtures. Coefficients are engineering assumptions pending prospective validation, not fitted or guaranteed returns.

Handicap quote adapter v2 corrects the pre-match provider convention: the numeric line in both Home and Away labels refers to the home team. Away -1.5 is normalized to away +1.5. Original observations remain traceable; historical pairs are reconstructed only at identical operator/provider/observation times under versioned ledger keys. Previous incorrectly paired handicap prices are excluded from promoted opportunities and priced audit returns. The repair workflow regenerates upcoming price readings from existing observations with zero API calls; started forecasts and statistical capture times stay frozen.
